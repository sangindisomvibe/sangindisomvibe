Culture, Heritage & Folklore

Learn about ancient instruments, sacred seasonal festivals, and timeless tribal traditions.

Tumdah & Tamak

The heartbeat of Santhal village celebrations. The Tumdah (earthen two-sided drum) and the iron Tamak form the rhythmic soul of community folk dances.

Folk Percussion & Echo

Dhodro Banam

A bowed wooden chordophone meticulously carved with human figures and floral motifs. Its haunting, warm tones accompany mythical narratives and ancient ballads.

Stringed Heritage

Sohrai Celebration

Celebrated during winter harvest, houses are adorned with stunning mud mural art portraying cattle, birds, and forest trees in homage to ancestors and fertility.

Harvest & Wall Murals

Baha Parab (Spring)

The festival of blooming Sal flowers and rejuvenation. Sal blossoms are distributed across the village, symbolizing harmony, renewal, and gratitude toward mother nature.
